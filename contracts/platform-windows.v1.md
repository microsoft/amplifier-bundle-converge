# Platform Contract — Windows — v1 (DRAFT)

**Who builds against this:** whoever builds the Windows body of Amplifier
Converge, and the intent steward who keeps it open on a desk beside the rest of
their work. It holds only the idioms this platform adds to `experience.v1`, the
umbrella every body keeps. An umbrella-plus-platform pair is the work of one
sub-project, with its own repository and its own steward.

## Purpose

The desk is where reading is long, several things are held in view at once, and
the Manager Console stays open beside the work. This body sits inside the
system's own furniture — its window frame, its tray, its notifications —
adding no behavior the umbrella lacks and dropping none.

## Core (the teeth)

1. **Everything the umbrella requires is present here, in this platform's
   shape.** The anchor is a manager session; the home is the list of them sorted
   by which needs you; Direction and Operation are the two places; the Manager
   Console is a pane beside either, never a third place; at most five things ask
   for your word; all five writes work — word, priority, feedback, steer, ask.
2. **What the umbrella asks of every body, this body keeps.** It holds no data
   of its own; whatever you can do here the manager session can do too; state
   words stay plain; evidence is shown, with technical detail folded; a proposal
   looks the same whoever wrote it; what changed since you last read is shown
   sentence by sentence; locking is gated and never automatic.
3. **Idioms satisfy behaviors; they never subtract them.** Everything below is
   shape: a difference is an idiom when you can do the same thing, learn the
   same fact, and be refused for the same reason — a divergence when you cannot.
4. **The window's own title bar carries the manager session and the needs-you
   count — IDIOM.** Which session you are looking at, and whether it wants you,
   is legible from the window itself without the app being in front.
5. **A tray icon is the quick-access surface — OPTIONAL.** Where it exists it
   carries the needs-you count and opens into the thing at the top; the body is
   complete without one, and it is never a place of its own.
6. **Notifications go through the system's own centre and obey its quiet
   hours — IDIOM.** A notification names which manager session and what kind of
   call, and opens into that thing rather than the home list.
7. **Manager sessions, repositories, and documents live in a side pane, and the
   Manager Console is a pane you drag — IDIOM.** Both stay in place while you
   move between Direction and Operation, at the widths you chose.
8. **Every write is reachable from the keyboard — IDIOM.** A steward working at
   a desk answers with a word without moving to the mouse.
9. **What this platform cannot do is said, not hidden.** Where a required
   behavior is out of reach, the body says so and names what to do instead.

## What v1 deliberately does NOT freeze

- The idiom set itself — the shapes above are the ones defensible today. The
  full set is settled with this platform's sub-project, against real use on a
  real machine; each addition is judged an idiom, never a new behavior.
- More than one window at a time — promoted when a steward asks to watch two
  manager sessions side by side.
- Reading while the machine is offline — promoted once the browser body's
  offline behavior has been lived with and found worth carrying here.

## Conformance kit asserts

- Every behavior the umbrella requires is reachable in this body.
- Five write paths, no more, each mapped to a named manager-session operation.
- The Manager Console stays live, and keeps its width, across a place switch.
- A notification opens into the thing it names, not into the home list.
- No idiom here removes a state, a refusal, or a fact the umbrella requires.

## Reserved / open questions

- Whether the quick-access surface should be able to answer with a word.
