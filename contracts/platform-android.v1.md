# Platform Contract — Android — v1 (DRAFT)

**Who builds against this:** whoever builds the Android body of Amplifier
Converge — phone, foldable, and tablet — and the intent steward who carries it
in a pocket. It holds only the idioms this platform adds to `experience.v1`,
the umbrella every body keeps. An umbrella-plus-platform pair is the work of
one sub-project, with its own repository and its own steward.

## Purpose

The phone is where "which manager needs me?" is asked most often and answered
in seconds. This body answers it with one thumb, in shapes this platform's
owners already know, adding no behavior the umbrella lacks and dropping none.

## Core (the teeth)

1. **Everything the umbrella requires is present here, in this platform's
   shape.** The anchor is a manager session; the home is the list of them sorted
   by which needs you; Direction and Operation are the two places; the Manager
   Console is a pane beside either, never a third place; at most five things ask
   for your word; all five writes work — word, priority, feedback, steer, ask.
2. **What the umbrella asks of every body, this body keeps.** It holds no data
   of its own; whatever you can do here the manager session can do too; state
   words stay plain; evidence is shown, with technical detail folded; a proposal
   looks the same whoever wrote it; what changed since you last read is shown
   sentence by sentence; locking is gated and never automatic.
3. **Idioms satisfy behaviors; they never subtract them.** Everything below is
   shape: a difference is an idiom when you can do the same thing, learn the
   same fact, and be refused for the same reason — a divergence when you cannot.
4. **The two places live in a bottom navigation bar — IDIOM.** Direction and
   Operation sit within thumb reach, where this platform puts navigation, so
   answering never asks for a second hand.
5. **Each kind of call has its own notification channel — IDIOM.** A steward can
   quiet one kind — say, everything but a decision waiting on them — using the
   system's own controls, rather than a setting this app invents.
6. **The system's own back gesture returns you where you were — IDIOM.** Leaving
   a sheet, a review, or an evidence drill-in never loses the place you were
   reading, and never drops you at the home list instead.
7. **The body is a share target for a screenshot or a voice note — IDIOM.**
   Feedback is dropped from wherever the steward already is, through the
   system's own way of handing something to an app.
8. **A home-screen widget or a quick-settings tile is the quick-access surface —
   OPTIONAL.** Where one exists it carries the needs-you count and opens into
   the thing at the top; the body is complete without it, and it is never a
   place of its own.
9. **What this platform cannot do is said, not hidden.** Where a required
   behavior is out of reach, the body says so and names what to do instead.

## What v1 deliberately does NOT freeze

- The idiom set itself — the shapes above are the ones defensible today. The
  full set is settled with this platform's sub-project, against real use on a
  real device; each addition is judged an idiom, never a new behavior.
- Answering directly from a notification — promoted when a steward asks to
  ratify without opening the app, and the risk of a mis-tap is understood.
- What a foldable or a tablet does with the extra width — promoted when either
  is in daily use.

## Conformance kit asserts

- Every behavior the umbrella requires is reachable in this body, on a phone.
- Five write paths, no more, each mapped to a named manager-session operation.
- The Manager Console stays live across a switch between the two places.
- Back from any sheet or review returns to the reading position it opened from.
- No idiom here removes a state, a refusal, or a fact the umbrella requires.

## Reserved / open questions

- Whether the quick-access surface should be able to answer with a word.
