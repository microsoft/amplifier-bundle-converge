Today I am turning your decisions about the experience into promises the
project can be held to, and proving the shape of the app while I do it.

The order matters. The umbrella promise goes first, because the two places and
the console pane are named there and everything else refers to them. The two
place promises and the platform promises go next, in parallel, because they
cannot collide once the umbrella has settled the vocabulary. The proof of
concept runs alongside all of them rather than after, so that a promise nobody
can build against is caught today rather than next week.

What I am deliberately not doing: drawing the real conversation into the app,
and attaching the console to a live session. Both wait on you — the first is a
decision you already deferred, the second on notes you have not sent yet.

If a promise turns out to be unbuildable, I will bring you the choice rather
than quietly softening the words.
