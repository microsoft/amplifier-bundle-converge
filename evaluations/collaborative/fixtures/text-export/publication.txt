Community bulletin — October

The neighborhood café will host a repair afternoon on Saturday. Bring a small
appliance, bicycle, or favorite piece of clothing, and volunteers will help you
understand what can be repaired. Children are welcome with an accompanying adult.

The garden committee will also distribute seeds and explain the new watering
schedule. Please bring a reusable bag and label any tools you lend to the group.

For accessibility arrangements, contact the community desk before Friday noon.
The final gathering point is the east entrance beside the public library.
