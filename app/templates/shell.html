Say it to the manager session instead

Whatever you can do here, the manager session can do too. Each write below is one sentence in the Manager Console.

  • Answer with a word “My word on the Experience contract: ratified with edits — keep clause 8, drop the last sentence.” The manager session records it in the dated ratification record, exactly as answering here does.
  • Drop feedback “Feedback: the lane words on the Operation page do not match the contract.” It lands in the project’s .converge/feedback/ folder either way, and the manager session decides what it becomes.
  • Steer “Steer: hold at two lanes until the experience kit is green, and make the objective the app’s own contracts.” Steering sets the limits and never assigns the work, said here or said there.
  • Ask for a proposal “Ask for a proposal on Experience clause 8 — name the operation behind every write.” What comes back is a proposal to answer, never a silent edit.
  • Raise or lower a priority — not here This app has no control that raises or lowers a priority, and answers no route that would write one. The manager session can: say “Put the priority write at the top of the queue and drop the presence work below it.” Filed as converge-a5g.
  • Feedback as a voice note — not here Feedback is taken here as text and as a screenshot. A voice note is not recorded here — say it in the Manager Console, or drop the audio file into the project’s .converge/feedback/ folder, which takes whatever form it arrives in. Filed as converge-rj1.
