# Cut-out Contract — v1 (DRAFT)

**Who builds against this:** anyone who fills the kettle, the person who
services it, and whoever writes the element's firmware. A silent change here
would surprise all three.

## Purpose

The kettle must not boil dry. Everything else about it can change; this
cannot, because it is the difference between a kitchen appliance and a fire.

## Core (the teeth)

1. **The element cuts out before the water is gone.** It stops with at least
   a centimetre of water left, so the metal never runs hot in air.
2. **There is no setting that turns this off.** A protection a person can
   disable is a protection that will be found disabled.
3. **It cuts out again on a refill.** A kettle switched on empty and then
   filled must be safe on the second boil as well as the first.

## What v1 deliberately does NOT freeze

- The exact centimetre — promoted to a number when a second element supplier
  exists and the two disagree.

## Conformance kit asserts

- Boil to empty with the lid off: the element cuts out with water remaining.
  This is the worked example of right and wrong — right is a cut-out with
  water left; wrong is any run that ends dry.
- Switch on empty, then fill: it cuts out on the second boil too.

## Reserved / open questions (NOT frozen)

- Whether a cut-out should also be said out loud.
