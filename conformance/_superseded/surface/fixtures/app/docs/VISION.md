# Kettle — Vision (DRAFT)

*A small project, written out in full so the page has something real to show.*

---

## Where this is going

Kettle boils water and tells you when it is ready. It does one thing, it does
it in under three minutes, and it never boils dry. Anyone in the house can use
it without being taught.

## Principles

1. **It never boils dry.** The element cuts out before the water is gone,
   every time, with no setting to get wrong.
2. **Ready is said out loud.** A person in the next room knows it is done
   without coming to look.
3. **One control.** There is a switch. There is nothing else to learn.

## What Converge deliberately does not do

Grind coffee · keep water hot indefinitely · connect to anything.

## What we have seen

Twelve households used it for a month. Two boiled it empty on purpose; the
element cut out both times. One person could not hear it from upstairs, which
is what the second principle now exists to fix.
