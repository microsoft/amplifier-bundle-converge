The halves promise — proposed change

Proposed here, in this project. The original stands until you answer.

The exact change
  • Every half is answered in one pass, or carried with a reason.
  • Every half is answered in one pass, or carried with a reason and a date.
The evidence

Three halves were carried in August with no reason recorded, and nobody could say afterwards why. A cost paid, not a preference.

What does not change

The one-pass rule itself, and every other sentence of the promise.
