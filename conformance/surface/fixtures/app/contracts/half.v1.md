# Saying Ready — v1 (DRAFT)

**Who builds against this:** the person waiting for the water, and whoever
chooses the sound.

## Purpose

A kettle that has finished and says nothing wastes the wait. Ready must reach
a person who is not standing next to it.

## Core (the teeth)

1. **Ready is audible from the next room.** Not a click, not a light — a
   sound a person hears through a closed door.
2. **It says it once.** A kettle that keeps announcing itself gets unplugged.

## What v1 deliberately does NOT freeze

- Which sound — promoted when someone reports the current one unpleasant.

## Reserved / open questions (NOT frozen)

- Whether it should say anything at all when nobody is home.
