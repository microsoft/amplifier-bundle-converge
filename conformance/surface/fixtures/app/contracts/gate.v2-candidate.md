# Proposal — raise the cut-out margin to two centimetres

target: contracts/gate.v1.md

## The exact change

In clause 1, replace:

> It stops with at least a centimetre of water left, so the metal never runs
> hot in air.

with:

> It stops with at least two centimetres of water left, so the metal never
> runs hot in air.

Nothing else in clause 1 changes, and no other clause is touched.

## The evidence

Unit 7 of the twelve-household trial cut out at the stated centimetre and the
element still discoloured — measured at 214°C at the coil, thirty seconds
after cut-out. The margin was paid for in a replaced element, not in a
preference. Two centimetres held the coil under 160°C on the same rig.

## What does not change

The centimetre is still not a setting anyone can turn off. Clause 2 and
clause 3 stand exactly as written. The conformance kit's two checks are
unchanged; only the number they check against moves.
