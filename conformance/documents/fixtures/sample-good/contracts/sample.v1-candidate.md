# Proposal — Sample Contract v1, clause 2

A proposal beside the contract it would change. The contract stays the law
until this is ratified.

## The exact change

Clause 2 currently reads:

> **One contract is about one screen.** Fifty to a hundred lines.

It would read:

> **One contract is about one screen.** Fifty to a hundred and twenty lines.

Nothing else in clause 2 changes.

## The evidence

On 2026-08-30 a real contract in this project was split into two files solely
to stay under a hundred lines. The split cost forty minutes and produced a
second file nobody has read since — a cost actually paid, not a preference.

## What does not change

- The requirement that a contract be readable in a minute.
- Every other clause, and the section order.
- The status of this contract, which stays DRAFT until the steward says
  otherwise.

## The ratification ask

Ratify, ratify with edits, or decline.
