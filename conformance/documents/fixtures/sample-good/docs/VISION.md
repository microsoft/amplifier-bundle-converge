## Where this is going

The project is readable. Anyone who joins it can read four documents and know
what it promises, who it promises that to, and how each promise is checked.
Nothing important lives only in someone's head, and nothing that matters is
discoverable only by reading code.

## Principles

1. **Documents are the through-line.** The same text a person agrees to is the
   text every session obeys. One text, two audiences.
2. **Checked against reality, continuously.** Every promise is re-checked after
   each change and reported in plain words.

## What this project deliberately does not do

Own another project's documents · ratify anything on a person's behalf · keep
history in a document meant to stay current.
